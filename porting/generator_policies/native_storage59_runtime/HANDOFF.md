# Portal query recovery

Three independent queries are complete. Reflected wrappers 0x1428fd6a0, 0x1428fd6e0, and 0x1428fe420 call 0x1415fc230, 0x1415fc250, and 0x141601350. The first returns X and Y from the vector at +0xa6c and Z from +0xa84; the second returns the three floats at +0xa78; the third tests bit 1 of the 32-bit flags at +0xaa0. The returned struct factory 0x142eea590 is FVector. Const is proposed from read-only behavior.

These fields are address aliases, not recovered original names. Storage at +0xa6c is a full FVector: 0x141601fae copies 8+4 bytes into +0xa6c/+0xa74, and 0x141602177–0x141602205 uses all three as floats in vector arithmetic. The other FVector is read as 8+4 bytes. Native activation writes +0xa84 as a float and adjusts it, writes +0xa88 as a float, and sets/clears bit 1 of +0xaa0. The field at +0xa88 also fills the actual slot between +0xa84 and the reflected lifespan. Full activation lifecycle and other flag writers remain open; do not substitute separate state.

Bounds and layout: parent Action registration 0x1428fa4a0 reports size 0xa60; AIPortal registration 0x1428fa5a0 reports 0xab0. Its reflected PortalDirection is FVector at +0xa60, PortalLifespan float at +0xa8c, and NoCollisionBuildings TArray at +0xa90 (16 bytes, ending +0xaa0). The proposed FVector +0xa6c, FVector +0xa78, float +0xa84, float +0xa88, and uint32 +0xaa0 exactly fill the indicated slots with no overlap. The registration thunk 0x1428fb800 calls constructor 0x1415e6c40, which initializes +0xa88 to zero, clears flag bit 0, and does not explicitly initialize the vectors, +0xa84, or flag bit 1. UObject allocation zero-initializes storage; the proposed plain fields preserve that and intentionally do not introduce field initializers. Constructor does not prove subsequent writers; the identified activation writes establish non-default behavior.

Evidence windows are SHA-256 pinned in native-evidence.json. No canonical manifest edits, builds, tests, or commits were made.
