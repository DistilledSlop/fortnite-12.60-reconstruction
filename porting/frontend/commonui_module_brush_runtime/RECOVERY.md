## Identity and lifetime

It roots that object, then invokes `1407d0c30` when its byte +0x28 is nonzero. Reflection identifies that byte as config `bAutoLoadData` and identifies settings at +0x30 as `DefaultImageResourceObject`, +0x58 as `DefaultThrobberMaterial`, +0x80 as `DefaultRichTextDataClass`, +0xb0 as `DefaultImageResourceObjectInstance`, +0xb8 as `DefaultThrobberMaterialInstance`, and +0xc0 as `DefaultThrobberBrush`. Shutdown removes the settings object's root unless `GExitPurge` is set, then clears module +8; there is no null guard before the ordinary unroot path.
