# Gifting info ownership investigation

The entry copy 0x142025400 is **not closed**: its GiftingInfo native shared member's pointee type remains unknown. `EntryCopy.pending.inc` records the observed field sequence and is deliberately not manifest-ready. A reflected-fields-only implementation drops ownership and is incorrect. Whole-entry assignment also incorrectly overwrites the fast-array base, ParentInventory, bIsReplicatedCopy, and bIsDirty.

0x142025370 copies the FString at +0, hero pointer at +0x10, and a two-word pair at +0x18/+0x20. It increments the incoming controller's strong count at +8 with a plain INC, swaps the pair into the destination, then decrements the old strong count. On zero it calls controller virtual slot 0, decrements weak count at +12, and on zero calls slot 1 with deleting flag 1. This is UE non-thread-safe shared-pointer ownership (ESPMode::NotThreadSafe); an opaque pointer, memcpy, or a thread-safe controller does not reproduce it. The controller scheme alone does not prove the pointee type or whether its declaration was TSharedPtr or TSharedRef. Do not invent a pointee type. Restore the native field through an explicit per-struct generator policy, at +0x18 with 16-byte size and normal C++ constructor/copy/destructor behavior, once typed payload is recovered. Preserve the existing reflected properties and their offsets; do not expose the native field as UPROPERTY. Ordinary FFortGiftingInfo assignment then implements the copy when its real declaration is supplied.

0x1420387b0 copies gifting info into an entry, derives bWasGifted from non-null HeroType and PlayerName length >= 2, updates its parent if valid and sets bIsDirty, then destroys the passed value. Its sole recovered direct caller 0x14259af20 passes a fully zeroed gifting value, so it gives no pointee evidence. 0x14295bef0 is a struct-array copy wrapper, 0x30-byte stride, and similarly supplies none. The next investigation must identify a non-null writer/constructor of the +0x18 pair and follow its allocation/controller virtual destruction to its concrete payload; neither the writer nor its address has yet been established. The runtime-generated/reflection metadata has no declaration for this native member, so the existing type cannot be inferred from reflection. 0x1420237f0, the entry constructor used adjacent to the zero-valued setter call, is a concrete next constructor to probe, although it may establish only the default value and will not by itself prove the payload.
